Background

Hey, I’m Amir. I recently graduated from the University of Maryland with a degree in Information Science. Growing up, I always knew I wanted to do something in tech, but I didn’t know exactly what. That changed during my senior year when I took a web development class that really clicked for me. It was so good that I even thought about changing my major, but it was a little too late. Since then, I’ve been learning on my own through platforms like Codecademy and Udemy, picking up new skills and building projects. I know that with the right program and guidance, I can grow into a strong software engineer.

Outside of work

Outside of work, I’m passionate about building community. I organize weekly soccer games with friends and members of my local mosque. For me, soccer isn’t just about the game—it’s a way to stay active, bring people together, and create meaningful bonds across different age groups and backgrounds.

I also volunteer with the DMV branch of AlMaghrib Institute, an organization that aims to make Islamic knowledge easy and accessible while empowering people to grow spiritually and personally. Every couple of months, I help organize seminars, lectures, and interfaith events designed to help people connect with their beliefs, learn from one another, and build stronger, more united communities. I’m involved in everything from setup to promotion and outreach, and I truly enjoy seeing how these events bring people from all walks of life together.
