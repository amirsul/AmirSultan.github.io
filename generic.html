DMV HEATS

I run a small reselling business where I buy and flip everything from electronics to sneakers.

I started reselling sneakers during my freshman year of college after my roommate told me how he bought a pair of shoes for around $125 and sold them for almost $300. That moment opened my eyes to the potential of reselling, and I decided to give it a shot. At first, it wasn’t easy. I had to do a lot of research, learn the market, track release dates, and understand how hype and demand work. Over time, I built a successful sneaker reselling business. I created a dedicated Instagram page that now has over 1,000 followers and built a growing presence across other platforms as well. I’ve sold to hundreds of customers, managed inventory, handled marketing and customer service, and even negotiated deals while staying consistent with school and other responsibilities. This business taught me more than just how to make money. It gave me real-world experience in entrepreneurship, time management, marketing, negotiation, and how to build trust with clients. It also showed me the value of consistency, discipline, and staying informed in a fast-moving market. What started as a side hustle turned into a passion that helped shape my mindset and work ethic.
